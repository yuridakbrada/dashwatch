Player completes Jumper in a version of Geometry Dash for Nintendo 3DS

Jadix120 catches the community's attention by playing one of Geometry Dash's classic levels directly on a Nintendo 3DS.

Published on August 30, 2026 at 00:34 · By yuridakbrada

Beating Jumper the way RobTop intended

Jumper gameplay on the Nintendo 3DS posted by Jadix120 on Reddit.

Playing Geometry Dash normally is already difficult. Playing Geometry Dash on a Nintendo 3DS, however, is another story.

Player Jadix120 posted gameplay of Jumper running on a Nintendo 3DS on Reddit through the gd3ds project, a homebrew remake of Geometry Dash created by AleFunky.

The project recreates the game's main levels from Stereo Madness to Theory of Everything 2 and also allows custom levels compatible with version 1.9 to be added.

The post quickly caught the community's attention, with several users surprised to discover that Geometry Dash could be played on Nintendo's handheld. Some players also reported using the same version and even importing levels from the 1.9 era.

Geometry Dash on the 3DS?

Although it is not an official version of Geometry Dash for Nintendo 3DS, the project demonstrates how the community managed to adapt the game to a completely different platform.

And apparently, Jumper runs very well on the handheld.
