cc478 reaches 14-100% on Eternal Silence after more than 400,000 attempts

New major progress marks one of the player's longest runs ever recorded on the level, as he now aims for 9-100% and the completion of Eternal Silence.

Published on August 29, 2026 at 18:16 · By yuridakbrada
cc478 records 14-100% on Eternal Silence after hundreds of thousands of attempts. Video: YouTube.

Player cc478 has made further progress in his journey to complete Eternal Silence, achieving a 14-100% run on the level. The progress came after approximately 281,000 attempts, in addition to another 135,000 attempts made on the rated version of Silent Clubstep, totaling more than 416,000 attempts related to the level.

The new run represents a recovery after a previous 14-78%. According to the player himself, the previous attempt was particularly frustrating, but the new 14-100% made up for it.

While describing his progress, cc478 highlighted that he did not have much bad luck after the 14-78%, having died at 74% or later only three times. In addition to the 14-100%, other progressions were also recorded, such as 24-100%, 47-100%, and 62-100% ×36.

The player now hopes to achieve new 9-100% and 73%+ runs, as well as finally complete what he describes as one of the most difficult levels of his journey.

“Fuck yeah, that's it!” — cc478, after achieving the 14-100% run.

According to the player himself, the 14-100% run is approximately as difficult as reaching 30% from 0%, and in his opinion, it is also slightly harder than Silent Clubstep.

With progress getting increasingly close to a completion, attention now turns to the next runs and a possible final attempt on Eternal Silence.
