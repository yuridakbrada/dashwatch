Eternal Blue is chosen as Daily #607 and receives Legendary rating

Level created by TheCubedGuy enters the Geometry Dash Daily rotation and receives one of the game's highest quality ratings.

Published on August 30, 2026 at 19:00 · By yuridakbrada


Eternal Blue, by TheCubedGuy, is chosen as Daily #607 and receives a Legendary rating.

The level Eternal Blue, created by TheCubedGuy, was chosen as Geometry Dash's Daily Level #607 this Sunday, August 30. The level received 7 stars and a Legendary rating.

In addition to the 7-star reward, players who complete Eternal Blue can receive 275 orbs, 13 diamonds, and 3 secret coins.

The level was originally created for the Duality Gauntlet Contest, a competition that served as the basis for its creation. Its selection as a Daily now puts the level in the spotlight for a much larger number of players in the community.

Eternal Blue also contains three secret coins. To obtain the first coin, the player must perform a long jump at 1.4% and again at 3.7%. The second coin requires the player to skip the yellow orb at 30%. The third coin can be obtained by performing a jump at 90.8%.

The Legendary rating also caught the community's attention. One player expressed disappointment that the level did not receive the highest rating:

“The fact that this isn't Mythic breaks my heart.”

Even without receiving Mythic, the Legendary rating places Eternal Blue among the levels recognized by the game for their quality and presentation.

With its entry into the Daily rotation, Eternal Blue is now available to the entire community through Daily Level #607, allowing players of different skill levels to experience TheCubedGuy's work.
