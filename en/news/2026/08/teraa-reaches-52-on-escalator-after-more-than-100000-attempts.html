teraa reaches 52% on Escalator after more than 100,000 attempts

Player records major new progress on the level, which is projected to land in the Top 10.

Published on August 30, 2026 at 10:47 · By yuridakbrada
teraa reaches 52% on Escalator after surpassing 100,000 attempts. Video: YouTube.

Player teraa has reached 52% on Escalator, marking a major new milestone in his current grind on the level.

In a comment posted alongside the attempt's video, teraa revealed that he had surpassed 100,000 attempts and said he expects to reach at least 61% very soon.

“100k attempts so faaaar expect 61+ really sooonnn”

Escalator also has a direct connection to Society: the level is a prequel to Society and was hosted by the same person.

The progress also caught the community's attention, with viewers making references to Popoff's history and to the level itself. One commenter joked:

“Popoff is gonna like this”

Another commenter directly referenced one of Popoff's famous quotes, adapting it to the new level:

“I’m a menace to Escalator”

The performance also earned praise from other players, who highlighted teraa's skill despite his relatively low profile:

“It’s crazy how underrated you are as a player. 52% is insane.”

In addition to the progress itself, teraa jokingly commented on the thumbnail used for the video, saying that he had no money and was homeless. The statement was clearly made as a joke.

With the new progress, teraa continues to push further into Escalator, a level projected to place within the Top 10 on the Demonlist. Reaching 52% has only increased expectations for a potential 61% or beyond in his next attempts.
