breakcore11 flukes Silent Clubstep from 40%

Estonian player completes Silent Clubstep after reaching 40%, recording one of the biggest recent flukes on the level.

Published on August 29, 2026 at 11:07 · By yuridakbrada
breakcore11 celebrates completing Silent Clubstep after fluking the level from 40%. Image: video footage.

Estonian player breakcore11 completed Silent Clubstep after reaching 40% during his attempt, achieving one of the biggest recent flukes recorded on the level.

Known for its extreme difficulty and importance in Geometry Dash history, Silent Clubstep requires an enormous amount of precision and consistency throughout its duration. Therefore, reaching 40% and managing to take the attempt directly to the end represents a particularly impressive result.

The player managed to overcome the remaining 60% in a single attempt, avoiding the need to perform new practice runs from the later sections of the level.

The achievement also establishes a new hardest for Estonia, making the completion even more significant for the country's community.

“HOW THE HELL DID I FLUKE FROM 40%? THIS HAS TO BE A DREAM, HOWWWWWWWWW? ON TOP OF THAT, NEW HARDEST FOR ESTONIA, LET'S GOOOOOOOO!!!” — breakcore11, after completing Silent Clubstep.

The completion marks another major achievement in the Geometry Dash community and adds Silent Clubstep to the list of levels the player has overcome.
